Product Overview

Marine AI Prediction Engine leverages satellite data (RRS) to predict chlorophyll concentration and analyze ocean patterns. Our advanced AI models process these signals, learn behavior patterns, and forecast optimal fishing locations to improve operational efficiency and sustainability.

How It Works

1. Data Ingestion

Raw marine data from satellite signals and vessel sensors is transformed into structured insights.

2. Model Training

Deep learning models using Temporal Fusion Transformer analyze historical trends and environmental patterns.

3. Prediction Output

Accurate forecasts aligned with real-world marine conditions support confident decision-making.
